# Amit Kumar

**Co-founder & CTO at [Nojoto](https://nojoto.com). Building AI for consumer tech.**

I build and run consumer platforms end to end: architecture, infrastructure, real-time media, payments, and the unit economics that keep them alive. I treat engineering as an optimization problem where latency, cost and reliability decide what ships.

## What I do

- **Lead engineering at Nojoto,** a vernacular creator and storytelling network for India, from first version to a production platform.
- **Design systems under real constraints:** media delivery, real-time voice and telephony, recurring payments (UPI AutoPay), and the cloud bill behind all of it.
- **Ship applied AI in products:** voice and speech synthesis pipelines, on-device content moderation, and AI workflow automation.

## Focus now: AI for consumer products

- Voice and conversational AI, including Indic languages
- Text to speech
- On-device AI

I am publishing the reusable parts as open source, with reproducible benchmarks instead of demos.
